Account

Delete your account

You can delete your Checkmancer account, made by Lucas da Paz (Mentalout), and the data tied to it whenever you want: in the game itself, right away, or by email, without the game.

In the game

  • Open Checkmancer and tap Options.
  • On the Account tab, tap More options, then Delete account.
  • Follow the steps and confirm. The account is deleted right away.

Without the game

Write to lucasdapaz@mentalout.com.br with the subject “Delete account” and your player name. If I need to confirm the account is yours, I'll ask for another detail about it, such as your level or the armies you own. The deletion is done within 15 days, and I'll let you know when it's finished.

What is deleted

  • The account: the game identifier, your Google account identifier and email, and your player name.
  • Your progress: level, XP, coins, gems, armies, achievements, Tower and Learning mode.
  • Matches, sessions and the device data stored with the account.
  • The purchase records stored on the server. Gems and subscriptions are lost with the account.

What stays

  • Online matches against other players stay in their history, without your name or identifier.
  • If you tell us why you're leaving, the answer is kept with nothing that identifies you.
  • Backup copies are removed within 6 months.
  • Google keeps payment records under its own policy.

Subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. To avoid being charged again, also cancel it in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

More details in the privacy policy.
