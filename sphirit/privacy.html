Privacy

Your data

Short version: I collect what the game needs to work and be fair, I never sell your data, I don't use it for ads, and you can have it deleted whenever you want.

Who is responsible

This page contains information about Sphirit.

What the game collects

  • Account data: an ID created by the game and the player name you choose.
  • Sign in with Google: I keep your Google account's ID and email. Google may also send your name and photo when you sign in; I don't keep those.
  • Device data: operating system, model, language and game version.
  • Connection data, such as your IP address: used on the spot to connect and prevent abuse, and not stored.
  • Game data: progress, matches, performance and play time.
  • Data about purchases made through Google Play: what was bought and the purchase status. Payment is handled by Google, and your card details never reach me.

Why

To provide the game (save your progress, play online, deliver purchases), to keep it secure and fight cheating and fraud, to contact you about your account (for example deletion, purchases and refunds), and to improve the game. Your email is not used for advertising or shared. The legal bases are performance of the contract with you and legitimate interest.

Items obtained improperly, including through refunded purchases, may be removed from the account.

Who sees it

In online play, your opponent sees your player name and match information. Data also passes through service providers that make the game work: the server host (Hostinger) and Google (purchases and Sign in with Google). Nothing is sold, the game has no ads and uses no third-party trackers.

How long

While your account exists. After you ask for deletion, the data is erased, and backup copies are removed within 6 months. Uninstalling the game does not delete the account.

The closed beta form (website)

It collects your email and the answers you choose to give, to invite you to the closed beta and send you news. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. The data is kept until development ends or until you ask for deletion; to stop spam, a scrambled version of your IP is kept for up to 24 hours. The site has no trackers or analytics; its only cookie remembers your language, and the page font is loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Sphirit is not directed at children under 13. If you're responsible for a child who created an account, write to me and I'll delete the data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data (including your game account) and withdraw your consent at any time. You can delete your account in the game itself, under Options, Account tab, More options, or by email (see how to delete your account). You can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD).

Changes

Important changes to this policy are announced in the game and on the site before they take effect.
